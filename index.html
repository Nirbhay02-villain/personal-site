About

Bridging business and technology

I'm a detail-oriented Business Analyst. I sit between business stakeholders and IT teams and make sure what gets built is what the business actually needs.

Day to day, that means running requirement workshops, writing user stories, BRDs and FRDs, mapping As-Is and To-Be processes, and testing the result through UAT. On the data side, I write SQL and build Power BI dashboards for internal teams and clients.

I studied engineering at NIT Raipur, hold a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt from KPMG, and I'm currently doing an online MBA in Data Analytics & Data Science at Manipal University Jaipur.
